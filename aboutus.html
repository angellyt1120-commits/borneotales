ABOUT US

Borneo Tales is a tourism website that introduces the beauty and culture of Kuching. We provide travel packages for tourists who want to explore nature, experience local culture, and enjoy their trip. Our goal is to make it easier for visitors to find useful travel information and choose a suitable package for their journey. The packages are designed for different type of travellers, whether they are looking for a short trip or longer holiday. Through our website, we hope to encourage more people to visit Kuching, experience its local culture, and create memorable travel experience.
